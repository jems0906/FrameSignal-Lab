## Recommendation

## Evidence

- Helpful: starts +1.50/household; return +6.00 pp (95% CI +1.64 to +10.36). Evidence insufficient to advance.
- Unrelated: starts +1.92/household; return -1.00 pp (95% CI -5.39 to +3.39). Evidence insufficient to advance.
- Misleading: starts +1.96/household; return -3.70 pp (95% CI -8.08 to +0.68). Evidence insufficient to advance.

Starts has 44.4% observed held-out sign agreement across only nine experiments (experiment-bootstrap interval 11.1%-77.8%). This is descriptive; noisy signs and constructed scenarios prevent surrogate certification.

## What Not to Conclude

This is fictional randomized presentation-policy evidence, not a Netflix retention definition, internal content metric, or claim that a show caused return. Completion among starters compares selected post-assignment populations. Positive household prediction is not causal surrogacy. No title receives an artistic-value score.
