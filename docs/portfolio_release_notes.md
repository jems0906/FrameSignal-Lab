# FrameSignal Lab v1.0.0: Educational Portfolio

This release freezes the completed synthetic FrameSignal-to-MetricSteward portfolio implementation. Review status is **self-review only**; no independent peer feedback or real-data validation is claimed.

## Included

- Household-randomized helpful, unrelated, and misleading presentation-policy scenarios; all assigned households remain in ITT.
- Metric cards, household uncertainty, held-out agreement, statistical diagnostics, known-truth operating checks, and separate exploratory follow-ups.
- One installed calculation library shared by FrameSignal, both research notebooks, and MetricSteward, with parity tests.
- MetricSteward's versioned educational evidence catalog, preserved scientific statuses, provenance, and real-world promotion guards.
- A case study, seven screenshots, a 31-second silent demo, and a reproducible local capture script.

## Findings and Boundaries

Qualified starts is rejected as a universal return surrogate; the held-out sign agreement was 44.4% across nine constructed experiments. Completion among starters is descriptive, not clean ITT. No candidate threshold, learned score, or observational method is approved for real decisions. No claim concerns Netflix internal metrics, title-level retention causation, or artistic value.

The original +4 pp coverage signal is retained and discussed alongside separately specified calibration follow-ups. Exact Monte Carlo coverage-proportion intervals do not certify the underlying Welch estimator or surrogate validity.
