# Independent Review Invitation

**Subject:** Request for independent review of a synthetic proxy-validation workbench

Hello,

I’m seeking an independent statistical review of FrameSignal Lab, a synthetic educational study of randomized presentation policies and longer-horizon household return. The project is not based on Netflix data and does not claim that any metric is used internally by Netflix.

The primary estimand is the household-level intention-to-treat effect of a fictional presentation policy on return during days 29-56. The study compares qualified starts and viewing days with that target, retains assigned non-viewers, and labels completion among starters as descriptive because starter status is post-assignment.

Could you review the estimand, household-level Welch inference, calibration follow-ups, multiplicity language, practical thresholds, and the limits of the experiment-level proxy-agreement results? In particular, please assess whether the current evidence supports only the stated educational conclusions and what should change before making any real-world calibration or surrogate-validity claim.

The simultaneous calibration follow-up uses fresh seed 20261102, 10,000 repetitions at each of five effect sizes, and nominal Bonferroni-adjusted Wilson intervals. It detects no familywise coverage deviation in this synthetic DGP; the report explicitly notes that this is not proof of calibration. No proxy is approved for real-world use.
